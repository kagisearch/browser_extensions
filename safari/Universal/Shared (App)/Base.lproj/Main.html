A little setup, then you’re ready to search.

  1. 1

    Enable the extension

    Turn on Kagi for Safari in Safari’s extension settings.

    You can also open → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Kagi for Safari. On older iOS versions, Safari is directly in Settings.

    Safari → Settings → Extensions → Kagi for Safari.

  2. 2

    Allow your search engine

    In the extension’s website permissions, allow the search engine selected in Safari, such as Google or DuckDuckGo.

    If it isn’t listed, search from Safari’s address bar, open the Kagi extension, and allow access to that website.

  3. 3

    Sign in and start searching

    Sign in to Kagi in Safari. Then type a search in Safari’s address bar to try it out.

    Open Kagi ↗

    Keep your current Safari search engine selected. The extension redirects supported address-bar searches to Kagi.

Set up Private Browsing Optional

In Kagi’s extension settings, turn on Allow in Private Browsing, if available.

Copy your private session link from your Kagi account settings. In Safari, open the Kagi extension and paste it into the private session link field.

Get your private session link ↗

You can also allow access to kagi.com to save the link automatically when you sign in. Regular tabs keep their own sign-in session.

To change redirect options or your private session link, open the Kagi extension from Safari’s toolbar or page menu.
